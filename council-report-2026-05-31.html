The Recommendation

  1. LexLocal — Romania only. Hardcode a directory of RO authorities (ITM, ANPC, ANSPDCP, IPJ Cybercrime, ANAF). Inject {LOCAL_AUTHORITIES_RO} into every existing prompt. No 35-language maintenance burden. Ship in a weekend.
  2. LexTimeline — priced at 3 USDT, B2B-positioned. One prompt, structured chronology output. Market dual-channel: scared consumers AND solo lawyers (25× ARPU upside).
  3. Outcome Feed (new — surfaced by the council). A /wins page with anonymous opt-in "result + amount recovered + jurisdiction" entries. Credibility, not virality. Reframes LexOn from "legal advice" to "tool that recovers money" — also regulatory cover.
  4. LexCall → reframe as pre-call brief. Same content, no real-time exposure. Ships in a weekend as a normal prompt tool.

Where the Council Agrees

  • LexLocal is the only universal pick — chosen by 4 of 5 advisors independently. The reason: hyperlocal authority refs are data-work, not prompt-work, and that's defensible.
  • Unanimous kill list: LexWatch, LexNotary, LexBundle, LexPanel. Each was rejected by ≥4 of 5 advisors and confirmed in peer review.
  • "One prompt + one config" is the design constraint, not a slogan. Three advisors warned half the list (Watch, Bundle, Notary, Case) are platforms-disguised-as-features.

Where the Council Clashes

LexShare — distribution gold or GDPR landmine?

Expansionist alone saw it as the 10× distribution engine. Outsider, Contrarian, Executor independently demolished it: scared people don't share legal trauma during the fight (only after winning), PII redaction is a month-long rabbit hole, EU GDPR exposure is real. The Expansionist is wrong about the channel.

LexCall — beloved by users, hated by strategists

Only Outsider and Executor endorsed it. Contrarian and Expansionist flagged real-time coaching as a regulatory landmine. Resolution: ship the prompt as a pre-call brief, not live coaching.

LexTimeline — retention trap or B2B unlock?

Contrarian and First Principles flagged retention features as CRM creep. Executor, Outsider, Expansionist all picked it. Resolution: build it priced as a stand-alone tool, not as retention glue.

Blind Spots the Council Caught (only via peer review)

  • The founder is not a generic legal-tech founder. All 5 reviewers flagged this: bilingual RO/HU oversized-transport escort with deep vertical knowledge (permits, bridge clearances, A.S.T. routes). The biggest move on the table is whether LexOn's next chapter is vertical depth in transport / haulage compliance RO+HU rather than horizontal "legal anxiety."
  • The regulatory ceiling is the real existential risk. ANPC complaints, BNR/crypto-VAT reclassification, MiCA 2026, Law 51/1995 unauthorized-practice-of-law exposure. None of the 10 candidate features address this. Three reviewers raised it independently.
  • The bilingual RO/HU stack is an under-priced moat. English-first competitors (DoNotPay) can't credibly cover RO or HU. None of the 10 features double down on this.
  • The verified outcome feed (only proposed by First Principles) is the only feature that compounds. Every win becomes acquisition fuel AND regulatory cover. Multiple reviewers reinforced this independently.

Advisor Responses (click to expand)

The First Principles Thinker — "the question is wrong"
A single prompt is copyable — no feature here is a moat. LexOn is a payment-gated wrapper around Claude Opus for legal anxiety. The real question is "why pick LexOn over free ChatGPT?" Three things matter: Distribution (you have it), Trust at moment of fear, Outcome over analysis. Only LexLocal (ChatGPT can't fake "Sector 3, form 24-B" credibly) and LexCase (returning users compound; switching cost) survive. The real moat nobody's building: a verified outcome feed. "127 Romanians recovered €43k via LexOn this month." Ship that instead of feature #11.
The Expansionist — "build LexShare, LexLocal, LexTimeline"
LexShare is the 10× distribution engine — anon "I caught my landlord stealing €4,200" cards on TikTok/Reddit in 35 languages, CAC → 0. DoNotPay never cracked virality because legal results feel private; make them feel like receipts. LexLocal is the B2B wedge — RO+HU mappings unlock municipal consumer-protection offices and unions with white-label deals. LexTimeline unlocks legal-ops — solo lawyers pay 50 USDT, not 2. Kill LexNotary, LexCall, LexVoice, LexBundle. The 11th feature you're missing: LexBounty — public scammer registry; banks, exchanges, Phantom become API customers. Data layer of fraud in CEE. Company-defining play.
The Contrarian — "the list is a graveyard"
Eight of these violate "one prompt + one config." Kill: LexWatch (liability bomb across 35 jurisdictions), LexCall (real-time = latency SLAs and user blames you when HR fires them), LexNotary (legally meaningless crypto-theater inviting notary-licensing issues), LexPanel (three prompts in a trench coat at 5× price), LexBundle (margin-eater), LexShare (GDPR landmine + nobody shares legal anxiety publicly). Build only LexLocal and LexVoice. LexTimeline and LexCase are traps disguised as retention. You're a transactional tool. Don't become a CRM.
The Outsider — "rescue verbs, not Lex-nouns"
I opened this and got tired by the third name. Lex-this, Lex-that. They blur. I cannot tell which one to click when my landlord just threatened to evict me. The one I'd pay 5 USDT for at 11pm with hands shaking: LexCall. Someone is on the phone RIGHT NOW pressuring me. A script on my screen telling me exactly what to say — that's rescue. Second: LexLocal (the actual phone number of the actual office in my actual city). Third, quietly: LexTimeline — my story IS messy. Kill LexNotary, LexBundle, LexPanel, LexShare ("I am NOT sharing my eviction story for your viral loop, are you serious?"). The list was written by someone who already understands legal triage. A scared person needs rescue verbs, not Lex-nouns.
